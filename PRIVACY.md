# Privacy Policy for IdeaVault

**Last updated: November 2026**

NexusMediaLab ("we", "our", "us") operates the IdeaVault Chrome extension. This policy explains how data is handled.

## 1. Data stored on your device
- Notes, categories, license status, and the source page URL and title of notes saved from web pages are stored only on your device, using `chrome.storage.local`.
- We do not operate servers that collect, store, or receive your notes. We use no analytics, accounts, or trackers.
- Uninstalling the extension deletes all saved data.

## 2. License verification
- Payments and license issuing are handled by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. The extension never sees payment or card details.
- When you activate, validate, or deactivate IdeaVault Pro, the extension sends your license key and an instance ID to the Lemon Squeezy License API (`https://api.lemonsqueezy.com`) to verify that the license is valid. Nothing else is sent.

## 3. Optional donations
- Voluntary tips are handled externally through Ko-fi. The extension does not integrate any tracking from Ko-fi.
